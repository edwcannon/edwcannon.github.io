About

Philosophy as a practice of meeting.

Ed W Cannon is an independent writer whose philosophical work examines interpretation, accountability, self-understanding, and the ways we meet and understand others.

His books were developed through sustained dialogue with ChatGPT, exploring what can emerge when human and artificial intelligence remain bounded, revisable, and accountable to one another while thinking together over time.
